Brand SVGs from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/1089fb7d2bf0e323f834c205ab76265005a6d5e8), commit 1089fb7d2bf0e323f834c205ab76265005a6d5e8.

All local artwork is rendered to lossless WebP at 128 px wide, with original colors and transparency preserved. The original monochrome artwork is retained in this format, along with the upstream CC0 license. Upstream SVG links below document provenance.

`n8n.webp` uses the n8n artwork from the same Simple Icons commit, filled with the brand pink (`#EA4B71`) for the toolbox.

The full-color WebP images in `color/` come from [Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.17.0/icons), using each technology's `*-original.svg` artwork. Their upstream MIT license is retained in `LICENSE-DEVICON`. The toolbox renders these local WebP files as images to preserve their original colors. The navy pandas mark has a light backing for visibility in both themes; MySQL and Electron are brightened in the dark theme.

`powershell.webp` is the plain PowerShell icon from [Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/v2.17.0/icons/powershell/powershell-plain.svg). Its upstream MIT license is retained in `LICENSE-DEVICON`.

`color/express.webp`, `color/github.webp`, and `color/vscode.webp` use the corresponding original artwork from Devicon v2.17.0. `color/dotnet.webp` uses that version's `dotnetcore/dotnetcore-original.svg` mark for ASP.NET. Express and GitHub receive a light backing in the dark theme so their black artwork remains visible.

`color/openai.webp` extracts the embedded OpenAI mark from the [ChatGPT badge in Josh's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jaesmade/jaesmade/blob/main/assets/badges/chatgpt.svg), using the badge's green (`#10A37F`) in place of white. The xlrd spreadsheet icon is a custom line symbol in `src/components/ToolboxIcon.tsx`.

The openpyxl spreadsheet icon and the Machine Learning, NLP, and UI/UX Design icons are custom line symbols defined in `src/components/ToolboxIcon.tsx`, not official brand logos. Their colors adapt to the site's light and dark themes.

`nmap.webp` retains the eye artwork from the [official Nmap logo](https://nmap.org/images/nmap-logo-64px.svg), with the wordmark and unused pattern omitted and the eye centered in its viewBox. It is rendered in its original colors. Nmap artwork is separate from the Simple Icons CC0 assets; credit belongs to the Nmap Project.
